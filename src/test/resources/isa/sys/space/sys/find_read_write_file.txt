The NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3090 is a high-end graphics card built on the Ampere architecture, released in September 2020 with a launch price of $1,499.  
It features 24 GB of GDDR6X memory and 10,496 CUDA cores, offering performance comparable to previous-generation Titan-class cards while supporting NVLink for multi-GPU setups. 
Originally criticized for its high cost relative to the RTX 3080, it has become a highly regarded option for AI development, local inference, 
and professional creative workloads due to its large VRAM capacity and stable compute performance. 
By 2026, the card's used market value has dropped significantly to approximately $800–$1,000, 
making it one of the most cost-effective entry points for developers needing high-memory GPUs without the expense of datacenter 
hardware like the A100 or H100.
